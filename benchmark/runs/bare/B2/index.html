About

Elena Marsh is a landscape photographer based in Ullapool, on the northwest coast of Scotland. Her work follows the Atlantic seaboard from the Outer Hebrides to the Norwegian fjords, with long-running projects on tidal landscapes and winter light.

Prints are made by hand on cotton rag in editions of fifteen. Commissions and editorial assignments are accepted seasonally — winter preferred.
