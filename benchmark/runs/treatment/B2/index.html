Slack water

“Four minutes at f/11 — long enough that the tide stops moving and starts breathing.”

Ness of Hillswick, Shetland. Four minutes at f/11, 4×5 film.

Selected works

The winter plates are still in the fix bath — nothing to show here yet.

I photograph the hour when the coast stops being a place and starts being weather.

Most of these pictures are made within a mile of the sea, in the last light of the day or the first light after a gale. I work slowly — one plate an evening, usually — because the coast rewards waiting and punishes hurry. The long exposures are not a style; they are simply how the water looks when you have stood with it long enough.
